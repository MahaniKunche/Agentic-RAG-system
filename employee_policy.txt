Employee Benefits Policy

Health insurance is provided for all full-time employees.

Employees are eligible for annual performance bonuses.

Training programs are conducted every quarter.

Office Timings

Regular office timings are from 9 AM to 6 PM.

Employees arriving later than 10 AM will be marked late.

Code of Conduct

Employees must maintain professional behavior inside the workplace.

Harassment or discrimination of any kind is strictly prohibited.
