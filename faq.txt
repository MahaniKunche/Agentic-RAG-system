Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How can employees apply for leave?
A: Employees can apply for leave through the HR portal.

Q: Is remote work allowed?
A: Yes, employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.

Q: How often should passwords be changed?
A: Passwords must be updated every 90 days.

Q: Are annual bonuses provided?
A: Yes, employees are eligible for annual performance bonuses.
