Employee Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 24 paid leave days per year.

Sick leave can be applied through the HR portal.

Employees must notify their reporting manager before taking emergency leave.

Work From Home Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.

Remote employees must attend all scheduled virtual meetings.

Laptop Usage Policy

Employees are responsible for maintaining company laptops properly.

Confidential company information must not be shared externally.

Security Policy

Passwords must be updated every 90 days.

Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all employee accounts.
